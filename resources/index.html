    Transitioning from GRA/TA to Fellowship¶

    Health Insurance: When you are getting paid through GRA/TA you will automatically get health insurance through the university. When you are on Fellowship you are in charge of getting your own health insurance but fear not the department covers the cost of this so you should get a roughly ~$2,000 stipend in September to cover the insurance.

    A nifty outline for getting health insurance can be found here.

    Payment: When getting paid through GRA/TA you will get paid at the end of the month. When you are on fellowship they will pay you at the beginning of the month. This means that when you are going from GRA/TA to Fellowship you will get "double" payed because you will get paid for the last month of your GRA/TA and then the fellowship will pay you at the beginning of the month.

    Note: When you are going from fellowship to GRA/TA there will be a roughly two month gap of payment since you will get payed at the beginning of the month for Fellowshipand then the end of the month for GRA/TA, so something to be mindful here.

    Example: If the Fellowship pays you for the Month of August but then in September you go to GRA/TA you will not get paid until the end of September. So you would need to keep this in mind for any major expenses such as rent.

    Graduating with Master¶

    Normal Process¶

    To get your master you need to fill out the necessary forms for CNS and compile your masters thesis into the necessary format required by the College of Natural Science. You can look here for more details about the deadlines to submit the Masters Thesis. In short some of the things you need to do is to be signed up for a specific class to get your masters. You should talk to the Graduate Coordinator or the Graduate Student Advisor.

    Mastering Out¶

    If you find yourself in a situtation wher eyou think that mastering out would be the right choice for you there is a way to Master out. The format for this is that you would need to:

    • Talk with your Advisor/Committee and the Grad Advisor
    • Finish the Master process
    • Submit your Masters Thesis
    • Fill out all the necessary forms for CNS

    How you master out is also important in terms of tuitions and payments of those. There is a timeline where if you master out before that time you will need to pay some of your tuitions. To figure this timeline out contact the Grad Coordinator for that info.

    Graduating with Ph.D.¶

    Academia¶

    You would follow the graduation guidelines that are outlined by CNS and the A-Z as normal. A small summary for this is: - Plan the Defence - Organize the close door session - Write up the dissertation - Graduate at the end of the semester that you planned the defence (Fall/Spring/Summer)

    Industry¶

    The path to industry is slightlly different than academia due to the quick nature of a job response once you get one. You are expected to start within a 0-4 week timeline. The best way to make your transition as smooth as possible is that you should accept a job sometime in middle of the semester. Some of the steps are:

    • Plan the defence talk and the closed door session. This will be something you will need to plan with youyr committee. Once you have done the defence and filled out the necessary forms you are more than ready to leave the university but note that you will not graduate until the end of the semester. If you need something for your employer to prove that you finished your schooling the Grad Coordinator can get that for you.
